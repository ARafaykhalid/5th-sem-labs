About the Event

University Techno Event is an annual technology festival organized to encourage students to demonstrate their technical and creative skills.

Our Objectives

  • Promote innovation among students.
  • Develop technical and problem-solving skills.
  • Encourage teamwork and creativity.
  • Provide practical technology experience.

Event Theme

The theme for 2026 is "Technology for a Better Future".

Did You Know?

Students from different departments can participate in the event.

More Information

Who can participate?

All currently enrolled university students are eligible to participate.

Is there a registration fee?

Registration is free for university students.
