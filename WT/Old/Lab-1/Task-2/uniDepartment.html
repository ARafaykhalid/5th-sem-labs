Department Introduction

The Department of Computer Science provides quality education in computer science and information technology. The department focuses on programming, software development, artificial intelligence, databases, networking, and research.

Academic Programs

BS Computer Science

A four-year undergraduate program covering programming, databases, software engineering, networking, and AI.

MS Computer Science

A graduate program focusing on advanced computer science, research, artificial intelligence, and data science.

PhD Computer Science

A research-based program designed for advanced study and original research in computer science.
